LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made and entered into this 11th day of June, 2026, by and between the Landlord, John Smith, and the Tenant, Jane Doe.

1. Premises: The Landlord agrees to lease to the Tenant the property located at 123 Main Street, Cityville.
2. Term: The term of this lease shall be for a period of one (1) year, starting on July 1, 2026.
3. Rent: The Tenant agrees to pay the Landlord a monthly rent of $1,500, due on the first day of each month.

5. Compliance: The Tenant agrees to comply with all local laws and regulations.

Governing Law: This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.

Signed,
